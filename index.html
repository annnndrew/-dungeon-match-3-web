How to play

Swipe from a tile toward a neighbor to swap resources. Match three or more in a row or column. A successful match moves your hero in the swipe direction, or attacks if a monster is in the way.

Find the key and reach the exit. On the final level, rescue Santa and bring him to the exit. The key and Santa are revealed from one tile away. Red tiles warn of damage on your next move.

When Magic reaches 5/5, tap its icon to choose a spell.

Turns. Refill your remaining turns. Every successful swipe spends one turn.
Health. Recover lost health, up to 9.
Magic. Tap the Magic icon at 5/5 to pick a spell.
Cookies. Collect cookies. The HUD shows your total collected across all levels.
Summoning. Matches of three, four, or five earn 1, 2, or 5 summon points. Every two points summon a level-appropriate monster after your move.
@
Hero. Your position on the board.
/
Guard / Warrior. The number at the bottom right shows remaining HP. Both types use teal tiles. Warriors have a larger character. Hunting enemy tiles turn red. Both types start with 1–3 HP on level 1, 2–4 on level 2, and 3–5 on level 3. Guards attack cardinally. Warriors also attack diagonally through open space.
/
Key / Santa. Collect the objective before taking the exit.
Present. Optional score bonus after it’s revealed.
Enter / Up. Your entrance to this level.
Down / Exit. Reach it with the key or Santa.
Attack. Damage dealt when you strike a monster, including a usable cascade bonus.
